My guess is that the headset needs time to switch from the high quality music profile into the lower quality call profile, and during that switch the audio engine delivers silence or nothing at all. So I would like you to add some logging around the moment the engine starts, record how long it takes until the first non silent buffer arrives, and keep that number in the local metrics without storing any audio or text.
